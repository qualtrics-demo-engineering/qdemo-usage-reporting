Robustness: the 1–2 year cohort (n=72, none ramping, full quotas) shows ρ = 0.32, p 0.005. Restricting to all non-ramping reps still gives ρ = 0.14 (p 0.009). Because Total Attainment = Quota Relief ÷ Quota (verified exactly), the signal is not a quota-relief artifact.
One honest caveat. The ≤1-year group's high 50.0% at-quota rate is partly a prorated-quota effect — newer reps carry much smaller ramped targets, so clearing quota is mechanically easier. The durable, defensible part is the within-cohort correlation (more demo usage → higher attainment), not that absolute percentage.

Editorial interpretation: QDemo's payoff concentrates in reps past onboarding but still building their playbook (~years 1–2), then fades. This argues for targeting demo enablement at the post-ramp window rather than a blanket rollout.

Our read (interpretation, not a study finding): high performers also far more often named getting in front of customers themselves as a driver of their success (33% vs 0%). We take that as a sign they were comfortable owning customer interactions — which in practice often includes demoing the product. The study did not measure demoing directly, so treat this as a supporting narrative, not evidence.
How much weight to give it. n = 28 interviews, so the usage gap is directional, not significant on its own (Fisher exact p = 0.125). The "repository" question covers demo content broadly — recordings and solution presentations — not QDemo logins specifically. And like the main study it is correlational. Use it to corroborate the tenure story and add a human voice to it, not as a standalone proof point.

Recommendation
Concentrate QDemo demo-asset enablement on reps in their first two years, and build demo usage into onboarding and post-ramp development. That is where usage and attainment move together. For tenured reps (4+ yrs), QDemo is not a quota lever — don't measure or push them on it for that purpose.
Population & join
AE+ES quota-carrying tiers only (451 reps). Joined to QDemo usage on work email (89% matched); reps absent from the engagement export are counted as 0 logins (50 reps), since that report only lists users with activity.
Metric & stats
Outcome is full-year Total Attainment (Quota Relief ÷ Quota; ≥1.0 = at quota). Correlations are Spearman ρ (rank-order, robust to right-skewed login counts); p-values via normal approximation given the sample size.
Why tenure, and what was ruled out
Across all reps the global correlation is weak (a blend of confounded subgroups). Cutting by tenure isolates a clean, robust signal. The newer-rep effect holds after excluding ramping reps and is not explained by quota relief. Region was examined and showed no reliable independent signal; it is omitted here.
