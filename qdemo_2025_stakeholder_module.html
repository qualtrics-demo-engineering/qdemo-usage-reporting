Full-year data tells a cleaner story than any mid-year snapshot. The rank-order correlation is statistically significant globally (Spearman ρ = 0.245, p < 0.001) and holds across all four regions. Spearman is reported alongside Pearson r because QDemo login counts are right-skewed — rank correlation is more robust here than a linear fit.
The 100+ login threshold
Reps with 61+ annual logins hit quota at 34.2% — 1.46× the rate of light users (1–30 logins, 23.4%). At 100+ logins the rate climbs to 41.5%. The rank-order correlation is significant globally: Spearman ρ = 0.245, p < 0.001.
Corporate outperforms, gap widens at 100+ logins
Corporate market units hit 31.7% at quota vs 21.8% for other market units. Corporate reps log nearly 2× as many logins on average (49.4 vs 25.1). The payoff is clearest at 100+ logins where corporate reaches 53.8%. Enterprise peaks at 37.0% in the 61–100 bucket.
LATAM and EMEA have the strongest signals
LATAM: Spearman ρ = 0.369 (p=0.027) and 29.6% at quota — small n, treat directionally. EMEA: ρ = 0.254 (p=0.012) with a positive trend. APJ: ρ = 0.29 (p=0.011), strongest in APJ Japan (r=0.34) and APJ ANZ (r=0.324).
NA Enterprise sub-segments diverge sharply
TMT leads at 21.1% QR (avg 17.4 logins). Goods & Services sits at 11.1% (avg 13.3 logins). Locations is at 9.5% despite 29.0 average logins. All three trail the 31.9% NA Corporate rate by a wide margin.
AE6 is the most concerning tier
AE6 reps average 45.5 logins — comparable to other tiers — yet only 16.2% hit quota. High usage is not translating to results at the most senior individual contributor level.
Enterprise reps have the most room to move
Other market unit reps average 25.1 logins vs 49.4 for corporate — and their attainment rate is ~10 points lower. Enterprise reps at the 61–100 login bucket reach 37.0% — above their own baseline of 18.8% for light users.
How to run this analysis going forward
Annual cadence at year-end is the gold standard — mid-year cuts are too noisy. Corp vs Enterprise segmentation is based on Market Unit name (contains "Corporate" = Corporate; all others = Enterprise). Report both Pearson r and Spearman ρ — login counts are right-skewed so Spearman is the more robust measure. To improve match rate from ~83% to ~95%+, switch the join key from full name to email.
